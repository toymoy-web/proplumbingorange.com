How tankless installation and service works here

Load and venting check.

We confirm gas line sizing and venting before recommending a unit, since older Orange homes weren't built with tankless in mind.

Sized to real household demand, not a flat one-size unit, so simultaneous showers and laundry don't starve the flow.

The Orange picture

Why Orange homes call about this

Very hard water.

Orange's municipal supply runs 16 to 18 grains of hardness. That mineral load scales the heat exchanger inside a tankless unit faster than in most parts of the country, which is why an annual flush actually matters here and isn't just an upsell.

Eichler tract slab systems.

Homes in the Fairhaven and Fairhills tracts have copper supply lines embedded in the concrete slab. A tankless retrofit on these houses needs venting routed carefully around that slab layout, not assumed from a generic install, and it's worth a leak detection check first if the unit sits near any existing dampness.

Older gas service.

Pre-1960s Old Towne homes sometimes have gas lines sized for a single old-style tank. We check line capacity before quoting a tankless unit that draws more gas on demand, since venting and gas sizing both fall under California Plumbing Code standards, based on the Uniform Plumbing Code, that don't bend for an older house.

Growing demand across the city.

Tankless service is now the single highest-volume plumbing search in Orange, ahead of drain cleaning and water heater repair, which tracks with how many older tanks in this city are reaching replacement age at once.

Not sure what you need.

"My water heater feels different lately," what that usually means

If your unit is heating unevenly, taking longer to kick on, or making a new ticking or clicking sound, that's not always a sign you need a full replacement. On tankless units specifically, this is often mineral scale narrowing the heat exchanger, fixable with a descale service rather than a new unit.

The choice.

Traditional tank.

  • Simpler install in most homes.

Tankless.

  • Continuous hot water on demand.

  • 20+ year lifespan with regular flushing.

FAQ

Questions Orange callers ask

Can a tankless unit actually handle a full household here?

Yes, when it's sized correctly for the number of bathrooms and appliances running at once. Undersizing is the most common reason a tankless install disappoints someone.

How often does a tankless unit need to be flushed in Orange?

We recommend annually given the local water hardness. Skipping it is the most common reason a unit fails early.

Do you haul away the old tank?

Yes, removal and disposal is included with every tankless conversion.
