The neighborhood

Santiago Hills at a glance

Where it is.

Santiago Hills is a planned community in the City of Orange, on the east side near Chapman Avenue, Jamboree Road, and Santiago Canyon Road.

How it was planned.

The community was planned within the area covered by the City of Orange's East Orange General Plan, adopted in 1989. The Santiago Hills II phase was approved in 2000.

The housing.

Homes here are far newer than those in Old Towne or the Eichler tracts. Because phases were approved at different times, we do not quote one build year for the whole community.

Your own home.

Your closing documents or county records show the year your own home was built, which is the number that matters for planning replacements.

Newer homes, different questions

Newer homes usually do not need the same conversations as a century-old bungalow. The questions here are about equipment reaching the end of its life, fixtures wearing out, and whether an upgrade makes sense. A good plumber helps you separate a repair from a replacement.

If your home is still under a builder's warranty, check what it covers before scheduling work. We are happy to talk through whether a problem looks like a warranty matter.

Worth knowing

A simple yearly plumbing check

Read the water heater label.

Note the install date or serial number so you know how old the unit is.

Look under every sink.

Check for drips, damp cabinet floors, or corrosion on shutoff valves.

Test the toilets.

A toilet that runs or refills on its own wastes water and usually needs a small repair.

Exercise the shutoffs.

Turn the main and fixture valves briefly so they are not stuck when you need them.

Water heaters are the usual first call

Many tank water heaters last roughly eight to twelve years, so a home that is now two or three decades old may be on a second or third unit. If yours is past ten years, leaking, or slow to recover, planning a replacement beats scrambling after a failure. California requires water heaters to be braced against earthquakes, and installation work follows the California Plumbing Code, which is based on the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC).

Our water heater installation and replacement page explains the process. If you are weighing a switch to tankless, our tankless versus tank comparison and our tankless water heater service page cover gas line and venting questions that decide whether it fits your home.

Fixtures, toilets, and disposals

In a home that is not old enough for pipe problems, the day-to-day calls are fixtures. A toilet that runs, a faucet that drips, or a garbage disposal that jams are all common and usually quick to fix. Our toilet repair and garbage disposal pages explain what we handle.

Local FAQ

Questions from Santiago Hills

Is Santiago Hills part of the City of Orange?

Yes. It is a planned community within the City of Orange on its east side.

How old are Santiago Hills homes?

We do not quote one year for the whole community because phases were approved at different times, including Santiago Hills II in 2000. Your closing documents or county records show your own home's year.

Should I replace a tank water heater before it fails?

Many tank heaters last roughly eight to twelve years. If yours is past ten, leaking, or slow to recover, planning a replacement is usually easier than an emergency swap.

Is a tankless upgrade right for my home?

It depends on your gas line size and venting. Call us and we will talk through what your home needs before you decide.
