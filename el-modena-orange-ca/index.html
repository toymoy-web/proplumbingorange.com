The neighborhood

El Modena at a glance

Where it is.

El Modena sits about three miles east of Old Towne, near the Chapman Avenue and Hewes Street intersection, around El Modena High School.

The history.

Quaker settlers arrived in 1883, and the town was founded in the late 1880s. It was also long home to a community of residents of Mexican heritage.

City and county.

Parts of El Modena were annexed by Orange in the 1960s and 1970s. The original town site north of Chapman Avenue remains unincorporated county land.

The housing.

The city's historic context statement notes that many small wood-framed bungalows survive, often modified, with newer bungalows and bungalow courts added later.

Homes that have grown over time

A house that has been in the family for decades often has a story of additions, a converted garage, or a bathroom added in a later decade. Each change can connect new lines into older ones using whatever was standard at the time. A plumber should trace how the lines connect before recommending a fix.

That is why we ask questions first. When did the addition go in, has the water heater moved, and where does the main shutoff sit? Those answers save time and prevent surprises.

Everyday repairs that add up

Most calls from homes like these are everyday ones. A water heater that is not keeping up, a drain that keeps slowing, a toilet that runs, or a faucet that drips. Our water heater repair and drain cleaning pages explain how we handle them.

If you keep paying for the same repair, say so on the call. A pattern of repeat repairs is often the signal that a bigger fix would cost less over time.

Water heaters deserve a special mention in older small homes. Units are often tucked into a garage or closet that was set up long ago, and whether a replacement can go in the same spot depends on the space, the venting, and the gas or electric supply. We confirm those details on site before recommending anything.

City or county? Confirm your address

Because El Modena spans city and county jurisdiction, permits and some services can differ by address. Before scheduling larger work, confirm whether your property is inside the City of Orange or in the unincorporated area. Our team is glad to talk through what to ask.

The names people use here

The city's historic context statement uses the names El Pirripe for the area north of Chapman Avenue, Hollywood for the area south of Chapman, and La Paloma for the hills south of Palmyra Avenue. We serve all of these parts of El Modena.

Local FAQ

Questions from El Modena

Is El Modena part of the City of Orange?

It sits on the east side of Orange. Parts are within city limits, and the original town site north of Chapman Avenue is unincorporated county land.

Do you serve both sides of Chapman Avenue?

Yes. We serve El Modena on both sides of Chapman Avenue.

My house has an addition. Does that change the plumbing?

Often, yes. Additions are usually tied into existing lines, so a plumber should trace how everything connects before suggesting a repair.

What are El Pirripe, Hollywood, and La Paloma?

The city's historic context statement uses these names for parts of El Modena. El Pirripe is north of Chapman Avenue, Hollywood is south of it, and La Paloma is in the hills south of Palmyra Avenue.
