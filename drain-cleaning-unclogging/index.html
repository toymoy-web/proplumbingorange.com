Orange's clogged-drain calls fall into two real categories: a single fixture backing up, and root intrusion into the city's older clay sewer laterals, especially in Old Towne. We clear both, and we run a camera inspection before recommending anything bigger than a standard snake, so you're never paying to guess.

Process

How we clear a clog and confirm it's actually fixed

Locate the clog.

Snaking or cabling first for a straightforward single-fixture clog.

Camera inspection when needed.

For recurring or main-line clogs, we run a camera to see the actual cause instead of guessing.

Hydro jetting for grease and roots.

High-pressure jetting clears the full pipe interior, not just a channel through the blockage.

Verify the fix.

We run water through the line after the repair to confirm it drains at full speed, not just partially.

The Orange picture

Why Orange homes call about this

Old Towne's original clay laterals.

Homes in Old Towne Orange, some dating to the 1880s, often still run on original clay sewer laterals now 80 to 140 years old. Root intrusion from the district's mature historic tree canopy is one of the most common causes of a recurring clog in this part of the city, which is why a snake-only fix often comes back within a season.

Wet-dry soil cycles.

Orange sits on a mix of clay and sandy loam that contracts during the dry season and expands with winter storms. That movement opens gaps at old pipe joints, which is a mechanical cause of slow drains that's specific to this climate, not just age.

Newer construction, newer problems.

Santiago Hills and other 1990s-built areas run PVC rather than clay, so clogs there are more often grease, wipes, or root intrusion at a single point rather than a whole-line issue.

Homeowner responsibility for the lateral.

In Orange, the property owner is responsible for the sewer lateral running from the house to the city main in the street, which is why we always confirm whether a clog sits on your side of that line before quoting a fix.

Mature street trees mean ongoing maintenance.

Old Towne's tree canopy is a genuine asset to the neighborhood, but it also means root intrusion isn't usually a one-time fix. Homes on original clay laterals often benefit from a jetting maintenance schedule rather than waiting for the next backup.

FAQ

Questions Orange callers ask

Will snaking actually fix a recurring clog?

If roots or a structural break caused it, snaking clears the immediate blockage but the clog usually returns. A camera inspection tells us if jetting or a repair is the real fix.

Do you offer hydro jetting?

Yes, for grease buildup and root intrusion that cabling alone won't clear.

Can you see inside the pipe before recommending a repair?

Yes, camera inspection lets you see the actual condition of the line before approving any bigger repair.
